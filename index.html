Introduction

An overview of Thavinhal Grama Panchayat

Thavinhal Grama Panchayat is located in Wayanad district of Kerala and comes under Mananthavady Block Panchayat. It is a beautiful highland region characterised by hills, valleys, plantations, agricultural landscapes and rich natural vegetation.


The region has considerable geographical and ecological significance due to its varied landscape and natural resources. Agriculture forms an important part of the local economy, with plantations and traditional farming activities contributing to rural livelihoods.


Thavinhal is also known for its cultural heritage, tribal traditions and scenic attractions. Its geographical characteristics make it an interesting area for studying rural landscapes, land use and local development.


This website provides geographical information about Thavinhal Grama Panchayat through an interactive WebGIS map, photographs and descriptive information.
